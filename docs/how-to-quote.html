How to quote

From the first call to a signed contract, in the quote tool. Open the Sales Portal and click + New quote. The step bar at the top shows where you are, and Back never loses your work.

  1. 1

    Customer

    Buyer's name and the state it will be built in (required: it decides what you can sell there). Email, phone and city if you have them.

  2. 2

    Model

    Pick the tab (panelized kit, modular, garage, hangar, steel kit, custom home), then the model. For steel kits and custom homes, type the numbers from the estimator.

  3. 3

    Options

    The defaults make a complete quote. Change only what the buyer asked for: how many, miles to the site, snow load, who puts it together, windows and doors (optional). Rare things (discounts, promo, already paid, add-ons and credits) are under Advanced. Every price change needs a reason.

  4. 4

    Review

    Check the buyer, the price and the payment schedule. Send a proposal makes the PDF for the buyer (a ballpark range is in the same box). When they say yes, click Write the contract.

  5. 5

    Contract

    Type only what the quote doesn't have: the buyer's full legal name (everyone who signs), mailing address and building site address. Check the payment schedule: it's filled in for you and prints on the contract exactly as shown. Change it only if the buyer agreed, and it must add up to 100% before Create the contract turns on.

  6. 6

    Send

    The contract appears. Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF (with our assembly crew, the Set Services agreement is there too). Have Sven approve it, email it or send it for e-signature as usual, then click Mark as sent. When it comes back signed, click Mark as signed here or in the portal. It only counts as contracted money once it's signed.

What the tool won't let you do (and what to do instead)

  • Complete modular ships to UT, ID, MT and WY only. Elsewhere, quote a panelized kit.
  • Wood kits (panelized kits, garages, custom homes) ship to UT, ID, MT, WY, CO, AZ, NV, NM, CA, OR and WA only. Elsewhere, quote a steel kit. A ballpark is still allowed.
  • Our assembly crew works in UT, ID, WY, MT, CO, AZ and NV. Listed prices never include assembly: pick Kit only, or Add our assembly crew (+10%, on a separate assembly contract). Outside those states it's Kit only. Only the office can override it, with a reason.
  • Steel kits sell everywhere, but outside the wood-kit states they're DIY only.
  • Windows and doors are optional on kits. Offer them only if the buyer asks.

Changing a contract: open the quote from the portal (Open or → Contract), fix it, and create the contract again: it updates the same contract until it's signed. After it's signed, changes go on a change order. Ask the office.
